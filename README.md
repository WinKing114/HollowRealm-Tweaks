# HollowRealm Tweaks

<img alt="Client and server" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Environment-Client%20%26%20Server-5b9c51?style=flat"> <img alt="Version 1.2.0" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Version-1.2.0-5b9c51?style=flat"> <a href="https://github.com/WinKing114/HollowRealm-Tweaks"><img alt="GitHub Source" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Source-5b9c51?style=flat"></a>

Spend Ancient Material at the Fabled Reforging Table and combine critical-hit bonuses from supported gear and Curios. HollowRealm Tweaks connects these systems across the mods in HollowRealm.

- Restores clams to shipwreck loot and the Bottle of Annihilation crafting recipe. Repairs affected gem bonuses and skips gun affixes when their gun mod is absent.
- Pack makers can adjust scroll drop weights across seven rarity tiers. Each spell stays within its allowed range.

## Installation

Requires Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47.4.16 or later in the 47.x series. On each character’s first login with 1.2.0, including existing characters, a one-time cleanup removes loose Patchouli guidebooks, the Ars Nouveau Worn Notebook and Enigmatic Legacy’s Acknowledgment from their inventory. Books inside an Eccentric Tome are preserved. HollowRealm supplies its filled Tome separately.
